✦ HONOURING OUR LEGACY

From First Flight to Future Skies

From four biplanes in 1933 to a modern, multi-role air force, discover the milestones that shaped the Indian Air Force.

✈ 1932 Year established
🇮🇳 8 October Air Force Day

1932
✦
THE BEGINNING

Four Aircraft. One Big Dream.

The Indian Air Force was established on 8 October 1932. Its first operational flight took place on 1 April 1933 with four Westland Wapiti biplanes, six RAF-trained officers and 19 Havai Sepoys.

Discover more

This small beginning laid the foundation for an air service that would grow through decades of training, operations and technological change.

1940s
✈
A GROWING FORCE

World War II and New Experience

During World War II, Indian Air Force personnel supported operations in challenging conditions. The experience helped expand skills, training and operational capability.

Discover more

The period helped establish a stronger professional foundation for the air force in the years that followed.

1947–48
🇮🇳
A NEW NATION

Serving Independent India

Following independence and Partition in 1947, the Royal Indian Air Force adapted to a new national role. Transport aircraft helped airlift troops to Srinagar during the 1947–48 conflict.

Discover more

Air transport became an important way to move personnel and supplies rapidly to areas that were difficult to reach by road.

1950
✦
A NATIONAL IDENTITY

Becoming the Indian Air Force

When India became a republic on 26 January 1950, the service dropped the prefix “Royal” and became the Indian Air Force.

Discover more

The change marked an important step in the identity of India's armed forces as a republic.

1965
⚡
TEST OF READINESS

Air Power in Conflict

During the 1965 India–Pakistan war, the IAF carried out air operations and supported India's wider military effort. The conflict highlighted the importance of readiness and coordination between military services.

Discover more

Lessons from the conflict influenced subsequent thinking about equipment, operational planning and joint coordination.

1971
✈
A DEFINING CHAPTER

The War That Changed South Asia

During the 1971 war, the IAF operated on the eastern and western fronts. Air operations contributed to the wider campaign that led to the creation of Bangladesh.

Discover more

The campaign demonstrated the importance of air support, coordination and the ability to operate across different theatres.

1999
🏔
ABOVE THE MOUNTAINS

Operation Safed Sagar

During the Kargil conflict, the IAF conducted high-altitude operations in demanding Himalayan terrain. Pilots had to deal with steep mountains, thin air and difficult conditions.

Discover more

The operation became an important chapter in the study of air operations in mountainous terrain.

2016
★
BREAKING BARRIERS

Women Enter Fighter Cockpits

In 2016, the IAF began the experimental induction of women fighter pilots, opening a new chapter in the service's history.

Discover more

The move represented an important milestone in expanding opportunities for women in military aviation.

Today
🚀
THE NEXT GENERATION

Modernisation and Indigenous Aviation

The IAF continues to operate combat aircraft, helicopters, transport aircraft and specialised support platforms. Indigenous programmes such as Tejas represent India's growing aerospace capabilities.

Discover more

Modern air power depends on trained personnel, maintenance, sensors, communications, logistics and technology—not just the aircraft themselves.

A LEGACY OF SERVICE

History of the IAF

1932

Foundation

The Indian Air Force was established on 8 October 1932.

1947

Independence

The IAF continued its role in India's defence and air transport following independence.

1965

Air Operations

The IAF conducted operations during the 1965 war.

1971

War of 1971

IAF air operations supported the military campaign during the 1971 war.

1999

Operation Safed Sagar

The IAF carried out air operations during the Kargil conflict in challenging high-altitude terrain.

Today

Modern Aviation

The IAF continues air defence, transport, training, disaster relief and aviation modernisation.
